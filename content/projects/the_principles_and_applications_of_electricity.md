This was the first project for EMS 1: make a short film only out of videos found on the internet. A resource was provided to us which contained a huge cache of old ephemeral videos — two main categories I noticed were those directed at women, about how to live a proper womanly life, and elementary school science lectures. I saw a connection between the two.
