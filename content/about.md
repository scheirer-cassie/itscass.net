I’m a PhD student studying Educational Communication and Technology at NYU Steinhardt. Inspired by my own educational journey, in my research I explore the design of creative inquiry-based learning environments that support empowering and critical engagement with the social world. I am particularly interested in understanding how young people develop passion for technological and scientific innovation, and how we might support that development through the design of accessible, playful, and artistic tools and spaces. Across my various creative and intellectual pursuits, I am inspired by critical theories that frame education, research, design, and the arts as mechanisms of social change.

Before starting my PhD, I self-designed my BA in Interdisciplinary Art & Media Studies and completed a minor in Human-Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon University. Beyond my studies at NYU, I work as an arts educator at the Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Youth Women’s Christian Association of Manhattan. In my (limited) free time I am an artist. I use a wide range of interactive, sculptural, and lens-based media to discuss queerness, childhood, and science and technology. I also love taking pictures of things in ways that leave you confused about what those things are.
