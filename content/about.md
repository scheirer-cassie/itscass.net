I’m a PhD student studying Educational Communication and Technology at NYU Steinhardt. Inspired by my own interdisciplinary arts education, in my research I explore the design of creative learning materials and the impact of these materials on youth sociotechnical literacy, identity development, and relationships. Across my various creative and intellectual pursuits I am inspired by theories that frame education, research, design, and the arts as tools for social change.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a BA in Interdisciplinary Art & Media Studies and a minor in Human-Computer Interaction from Carnegie Mellon University. Beyond my studies at NYU, I work as an arts educator at the Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Youth Women’s Christian Association of Manhattan. In my (limited) free time I am an artist. I use a wide range of interactive, sculptural, and lens-based media to discuss queerness, childhood, and science and technology. I also love taking pictures of things in ways that leave you confused about what those things are.
