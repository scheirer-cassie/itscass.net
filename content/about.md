I’m a PhD student studying Educational Communication and Technology at NYU Steinhardt. Inspired by my own interdisciplinary arts education, in my research I explore the design of creative inquiry-based learning environments that support empowering and critical engagement with the social world. I am particularly interested in how young people construct identities and relationships with and through technology, and how we might support that process by ethically designing accessible, playful, and/or artistic systems and spaces. Across my various creative and intellectual pursuits, I am inspired by theories that frame education, research, design, and the arts as tools for social change.

Before starting my PhD, I self-designed my BA in Interdisciplinary Art & Media Studies and completed a minor in Human-Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon University. Beyond my studies at NYU, I work as an arts educator at the Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Youth Women’s Christian Association of Manhattan. In my (limited) free time I am an artist. I use a wide range of interactive, sculptural, and lens-based media to discuss queerness, childhood, and science and technology. I also love taking pictures of things in ways that leave you confused about what those things are.
